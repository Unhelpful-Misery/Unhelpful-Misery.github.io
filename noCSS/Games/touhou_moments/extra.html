Thoughts

At first, I did not realise how much more memorisation was required to complete the extra stages compared to the regular game. Once I got adjusted to that fact, then the extra stages became a lot more fun. But also, don't expect amazing gameplay here. I allowed myself to beat the Extra Stages using any means necessary - bombspamming and cheese included!! Unfortunately, that means that I don't think I'll ever really sit down to play an extra stage for the funsies every once in a while, since it kind of has to be a big session.

Touhou 6: Embodiment of Scarlet Devil

Flandre Scarlet!!! How could I call myself a Touhou fan if I haven't even fought her properly? Also, I did not realise how much damage Marisa A could deal before I needed that DPS against Flan.

Touhou 12: Undefined Fantastic Object

I don't know if it's just me, but I was feeling pretty frustrated with this extra stage compared to the other ones. Maybe I just don't like difficult stage sections where I retry over and over again for a "good run," even if the boss is really easy.

Touhou 13: Ten Desires

I ended up actually really liking this boss fight. I don't know why; usually I'm not a big fan of custom bullets that only show up once, but I can kind of fairly naturally judge the hitboxes of the birds and the dogs. Also, the nonspells are so incredibly pretty, and I'm a real sucker for pretty attacks.

Touhou 14: Double Dealing Character

It's a pretty easy extra stage, but I like it! Sakuya's bomb remains basically unnerfed even though bombs aren't supposed to be as effective on extra stages, so that's big news for a bombspammer like me! The one gripe I might have is that ridiculous amount of screenshake on one of the attacks.

Touhou 15: Legacy of Lunatic Kingdom

Somehow, even with Reisen's cartoonishly large number of resource, this extra stage still proved to be quite a challenge... Personally, though, I loved this extra stage. There's something so exhilarating about having to try your very hardest to dodge insane attacks. Someday I'll revisit it with different shot types, but today is not that day.

Touhou 16: Hidden Star in Four Seasons

Really easy Extra Stage if you pick homing Reimu, because the damage you deal to the enemies that come behind you just becomes insane. Maybe I'll try it with other characters too, sometime in the future when I'm a better Touhou player.

I dunno about most people, but I felt like this was the easiest extra stage provided that you got a good card pickup and you've beaten the normal mode using a blank card. Having three starting cards is just really useful in this extra stage.

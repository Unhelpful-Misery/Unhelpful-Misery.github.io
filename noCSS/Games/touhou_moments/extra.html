Thoughts

At first, I did not realise how much more memorisation was required to complete the extra stages compared to the regular game. Once I got adjusted to that fact, then the extra stages became a lot more fun. Unfortunately, that means that I don't think I'll ever really sit down to play an extra stage for the funsies every once in a while, since it kind of has to be a big session.

Touhou 7: Perfect Cherry Blossom

Probably one of the easiest extra stages, if I'm to be completely honest. Just . . . Don't go into this extra stage DIRECTLY after beating the game for the first time in normal mode, or you'll be quite surprised.

Touhou 16: Hidden Star in Four Seasons

Really easy Extra Stage if you pick homing Reimu, because the damage you deal to the enemies that come behind you just becomes insane. Maybe I'll try it with other characters too, sometime in the future when I'm a better Touhou player.

Touhou 18: Unconnected Marketeers

I dunno about most people, but I felt like this was the easiest extra stage provided that you got a good card pickup and you've beaten the normal mode using a blank card. Having three starting cards is just really useful in this extra stage.

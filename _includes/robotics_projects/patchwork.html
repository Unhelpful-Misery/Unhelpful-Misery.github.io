Patchwork was a fun project to finally construct something really cool once I finally had the budget to start making things properly!

It's a mobile base that can have various items attached on top of it, like a big box to carry my stuff!

Patchwork is named like that because I accidentally bought two slightly different shades of grey PLA filament when creating Patchwork, so different parts are slightly different colours.

Thoughts

I know bullet hells aren't for everybody, but I personally love the Touhou games I've played so far. There's just something so amazing about that moment when you perfectly dodge and weave through an attack that you initially thought was impossible. That being said, I'm still a terrible Touhou player and very much a bomb-spammer, though.

Touhou 6: Embodiment of Scarlet Devil

Going back to the classics! I'll admit, this game is... Not really as fun as it could be, since it's missing so many QoL features. Plus, the incredible amount of RNG in the attacks can be quite frustrating. But, since it's the first game, I'll give it a pass on all of that!

Touhou 7: Perfect Cherry Blossom

I personally believe that PCB is the best beginner Touhou game, excluding how difficult it is to actually obtain the game. Maybe I'm just saying that because it was the first Touhou game I played, though. In any case, even though it's really easy, PCB's also really fun and the attack patterns are super pretty. I think PCB has a special place in a lot of people's hearts and it definitely has a special place in mine.

Touhou 8: Imperishable Night

I'll be honest, I never really understood the "Time" mechanic really well - sometimes I think I got a lot of score points and it advances by a full hour, and other times I think I did horribly and the time only advances 30 minutes. Anyway, I've learned to mostly ignore the time when playing my runs, since I don't use continues anyway. The best part about Imperishable Night has got to be the fight against Marisa. There's something so awesome about having to dodge Master Spark. Although, I will say, all of the boss fights are made significantly easier when you have, like, 2 business days to death bomb if you're playing as Reimu.

Touhou 10: Mountain of Faith

It took a WHILE to get adjusted to the fact that bombing meant my power went down, but I slowly started to realise how generous Touhou 10 is with giving you power items. Kanako seems really difficult at first, but once you start understanding her attacks, they become actually pretty fun. (except for her last one).

Touhou 11: Subterranean Animism

Even after hearing about the difficulty of Touhou 11, I still underestimated it. This game can be really harsh and really unforgiving, especially if you make any mistakes fairly early on. And unlike Touhou 10, the power-based bombing system means that you'll seriously feel the cost of bombing unnecessarily a lot of the time. Despite all of this, I do think Touhou 11 is a really fun game. It embodies the Touhou spirit very well.

Touhou 12: Undefined Fantastic Object

Honestly, I heard really bad things about UFO, but when I played it for myself, I didn't think the gimmick was actually that bad! It feels nice to have a bunch of lives with which to tackle bosses, but it doesn't feel like Touhou 17 where the gimmick completely trivialises the boss sections.

Touhou 13: Ten Desires

Ten Desires was interesting to play through. I really liked the music during some sections and the attacks were generally really pretty too. Overall, though, I feel like Ten Desires was just another game in the series, and wasn't really too standout? Maybe that's just because the main gimmick wasn't really that interesting, although PCB's gimmick isn't really that interesting either.

I will say, however, Youmu's attack is *really* fun to wield.

Touhou 14: Double Dealing Character

I absolutely love the gimmick of Touhou 14 - trying to figure out whether or not to risk going up to the PoC to get a ridiculous amount of resources is pretty fun. Also, the attack patterns get really difficult, but I also find them really interesting and Touhou 14 is really generous with bombs and lives if you play things right, so it's an appreciated entry into the seiries! I think with some time, it might become one of my favourites!

Touhou 15: Legacy of Lunatic Kingdom

The Touhou game which has probably indisputably the hardest attack patterns to dodge. Reisen's bomb carried me through this thing, and even then, I lost so many resources to Clownpiece. I hope that someday I'll be able to clear normal mode using Reimu or Marisa. At the same time, I do think LoLK is pretty fun, especially with Reisen's bomb because you always are at least trying to dodge the attacks.

Touhou 16: Hidden Star in Four Seasons

I quite enjoyed Touhou 16. It's a pretty standard touhou game quite late in the series with nice bullet patterns, and the gimmick is just a bit of a temporary shield. I will say, I definitely do wish the bullet visibility wasn't so bad.

Touhou 17: Wily Beast and Weakest Creature

I'll be honest, I'm not really *that* big of a fan of this gimmick? It's definitely interesting, but I feel like the spirits kind of invalidate dodging a lot of the time. Like, if I pay enough attention to otter spirits, then there's no need to actually pay attention to the enemy's attack, especially if I bomb to get the spirits.

Touhou 18: Unconnected Marketeers

I know the roguelike card mechanic of Touhou 18 is something that people either love or hate, but I absolutely love this mechanic. Even if it is a bit difficult to clear for the first time, the kinds of runs that you can have on your repeat playthroughs are just so much fun.

Touhou 20: Fossilized Wonders

I'll be honest, I don't really like Touhou 20 that much . . . While the different gemstones and stuff are fine and actually fairly interesting, I don't like how the difficulty in attacks is balanced out by the quantity of bombs you get. It just forces me to bomb through most of the attacks which I don't find very fun.
